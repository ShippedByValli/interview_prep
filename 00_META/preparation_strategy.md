# Preparation Strategy

# Philosophy

The goal is not exhaustive theoretical coverage.
The goal is:
- interview execution
- rapid retrieval
- structured communication
- decision-making fluency
- ambiguity handling

---

# Strategic Priorities

## 1. Python Fluency
Primary short-term bottleneck.

Focus:
- Pandas
- product analytics workflows
- data manipulation speed
- notebook fluency

## 2. Modeling Communication
Focus on:
- framing
- assumptions
- tradeoffs
- interpretation
- business relevance

## 3. Product Thinking
Need stronger:
- metric reasoning
- ecosystem awareness
- guardrail thinking
- tradeoff discussions

## 4. Experimentation Rigor
Deep focus on:
- trustworthiness
- SRM
- novelty effects
- triggering
- randomization
- causal reasoning

## 5. Communication Structure
Use consistent flow:
1. Clarify
2. Define assumptions
3. Outline approach
4. Discuss tradeoffs
5. Recommend next steps

# Learning Principles

## Compression Over Completeness
Prefer:
- heuristics
- one-page summaries
- frameworks
- mental models

## Active Practice Over Passive Reading
Prioritize:
- mock cases
- coding practice
- verbal articulation
- timed drills

## Retrieval Over Storage
Notes should optimize:
- fast revision
- interview recall
- structured explanation
