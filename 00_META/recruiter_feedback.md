# Recruiter Feedback

## Key Signals

### 1. Big-Picture Thinking
Need stronger top-down framing before diving into technical details.

### 2. Clarifying Questions
Need to ask more clarifying questions before solving.

### 3. Structured Communication
Answers should follow a structured flow:
- clarify
- define assumptions
- outline approach
- discuss tradeoffs
- conclude with recommendation

### 4. Product Thinking
Need stronger focus on:
- business impact
- ecosystem effects
- tradeoffs
- product metrics

### 5. Modeling Communication
Avoid focusing only on model metrics.
Need broader framing:
- business objective
- operational constraints
- interpretability
- deployment tradeoffs

### 6. Ambiguity Handling
Interviewers likely evaluating:
- reasoning quality
- prioritization
- ability to structure vague problems

---

# Action Items

- Slow down before solving
- Ask clarifying questions first
- Think in frameworks
- Improve concise articulation
- Tie technical answers to business impact
- Discuss assumptions explicitly
